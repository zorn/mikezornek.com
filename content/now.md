## Side Projects

Last month I delivered my goals of LocalCents and a demonstrable scenario of using Automerge. I [blogged](/posts/2026/8/local-cents/) about the project in detail and won't repeat myself here. Suffice to say I was happy to complete that research scope and am thinking about what's next.

I'm considering moving forward and contining to evolve LocalCents into a small business accounting app -- but before I get too deep, I'm trying to do product research and customer interviews to understand the landscape better. While that is going on, I may work on some general tech challenges, but I want to postpone the actual product design until I have more learning under my belt.

I've been keeping up with maintenance updates for [Flick](https://github.com/zorn/flick/commits/main/), but no real feature work. I do want to study up on observability and use Flick as a test bed for it. This is something I wanted to do this summer, but I chose to focus on LocalCents exclusively to make sure I saw it through to completion. With some open time, I may return to this in the fall.
