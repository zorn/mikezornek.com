Taking a quick moment to celebrate some positive habits that have landed for me. 

For the last 6 weeks, I've been building out weekly plans to keep me focused on the things I need to do to move important personal and professional projects  forward. I've been doing quarterly plans on and off for the last few years, but this is the first time I've been able to keep up with weekly plans. In addition to planning the week ahead, these also give me space to celebrate the accomplishments of the week that has past (which I think is important for mental health).

Those are practices that come out of the MacSparky [Productive Field Guide](https://www.macsparky.com/pfg/) which recommends a process of doing deep internal introspection in understanding your roles and the person you want to be, ultimately resulting in projects (and plans) that support those roles. I think its a great way to think about yourself and over the last few years I've been incorporating it more and more into my normal flow. There are some things David recommends like hyper scheduling your day that have not implemented (and I doubt will ever since I don't work like that), but I think getting more specific with my weekly plan has helped keep me centered as random distractions grab me day to day. 

Also, for the last 4 weeks, I've been keeping a signal log to track consulting outreach initiatives, cataloging numbers across a number of platforms and spaces. With intent I am not treating these as vanity number but more so understanding over time what topics people are most interested in and which community spaces are drawing the most visits (and thus might be worth more of my time as a participant). Some venues numbers like Reddit have been curiously high. I generate the report via Claude Code through a mix of API calls to my Plausable account and sharing manual screenshots of places it has trouble seeing. It feels like a good tool choice given the wide scope of sites whose numbers I want to see week to week. Not sure I'll keep this going forever. Ideally I land consulting work that demands more of my time, in which I still hope to blog but with less concern of converting traffic into leads.

To close, if you are not journalling I do encourage you to experiment. I think it can really help you defog ideas and conserations and keep you focused on your long term goals against an ever distracting world.
