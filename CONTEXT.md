# mikezornek.com

Personal Hugo site and blog for Mike Zornek. This glossary captures terms
specific to how the site's content is organized and presented.

## Language

**Start Here list**:
A hand-selected, hand-ranked set of blog posts presented as a newcomer's
on-ramp. Its bar is not "my deepest work" but "my most trust-building technical
work": posts that are both approachable and impressive to a first-time visitor,
chosen to earn enough trust that they follow and eventually join the mailing
list. It is a single flat list, not themed collections.
_Avoid_: Featured, Best of, Selected, Favorites, Highlights.

**Start Here entry**:
One item in the Start Here list: a reference to a post plus an optional
editorial note framing why a newcomer should read it.

**Editorial note**:
A short, author-written sentence attached to a Start Here entry that tells the
reader why the post is worth their time or where it fits. Distinct from a post's
own `description` front matter, which is the post's general summary.

**Social card**:
The landscape image a page shares with when its link is posted somewhere. It is
the reader's first impression of a link, seen before the page itself, and it
belongs to the venue rather than to the site. Every page has one.
_Avoid_: OG image, preview image, thumbnail. "Thumbnail" in particular already
means something else here: a small image inside a post.

**Generated card**:
A social card built from the page's own title and description, so it says what
that specific page is about. The default for every page.

**Static card**:
The one designed social card that stands in wherever a generated card is not
available. Deliberately drawn as part of the same visual family as the generated
cards, so falling back to it does not read as landing on a different site.

**Authored image**:
An image a page supplies for itself, rather than accepting a generated card. An
authored image always wins, on the grounds that someone chose it on purpose. It
is the per-page escape hatch from generation.

**Brand lockup**:
The small recurring identity mark carried on every social card. It is what ties
the generated and static cards to each other and to the site, and it is the only
element that does not change from card to card.
